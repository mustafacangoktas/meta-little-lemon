About Little Lemon

Based in Chicago, Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist.


The restaurant was founded by two Italian brothers, Mario and Adrian, who moved to the United States to pursue their shared passion for hospitality and great culinary traditions.


To craft the menu, Mario relies on family recipes and his experience as a chef in Italy. Adrian leads the marketing effort and manages the front-of-house operations.
